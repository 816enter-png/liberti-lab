Questions

What does a sign cost?

Signs start at $300. The final price depends on size, material, and lighting, and you'll get it with your mockup, before you commit to anything.

Can it go outside?

Yes. Outdoor signs are printed in ASA, a UV-stable material made to hold up to sun and weather.

Can it light up?

Yes. We build lighting into the print itself: LED channels, color-changing light, and hidden wiring.

How big can you go?

Up to 800 × 800 × 1000 mm in a single piece, with no seams. For anything larger, send the size and we'll work out the approach.

Do I need a logo file?

No. A link to your website or Instagram is enough. If you have a logo file or a photo of your current sign, email it to hello@liberti-lab.com.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size and finish. Your mockup comes with a timeline, so you'll know before you approve.
